# Tasks

The four features from the README. Each one links to its own plan in [tasks/](tasks/).

## 1. Sign-in

Replace the fixed tokens with real sign-in. Each user signs in as themselves in the app and on the dashboard, and stays signed in until they sign out.

Details: [tasks/01-sign-in.md](tasks/01-sign-in.md)

## 2. Live updates

New measurements show up on the office dashboard as soon as they're captured, with no reload. After a dropped connection the dashboard catches up with nothing missing or repeated, and users only see measurements from their own projects.

Details: [tasks/02-live-updates.md](tasks/02-live-updates.md)

## 3. Who's online

The dashboard shows who has the app or dashboard open right now, limited to people who share a project with you. Each person appears once however many devices they use, and a device that disappears drops off within about a minute.

Details: [tasks/03-whos-online.md](tasks/03-whos-online.md)

## 4. CSV export

A dashboard button that downloads your projects' measurements as a CSV that opens cleanly in Excel and Google Sheets. The values match the app and work in spreadsheet formulas, and the export works on desktop and on a phone.

Details: [tasks/04-csv-export.md](tasks/04-csv-export.md)
